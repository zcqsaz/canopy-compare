# Data and image credits

NSF's National Ecological Observatory Network operated the cameras. The PhenoCam Network archives their images. The Ecological Forecasting Initiative assembled the dated greenness and redness targets. This viewer pairs selected nearest-to-noon archive frames and downsizes them to a maximum of 640 × 480 pixels. Image timestamps are shown as encoded in the camera archive filename. The pictures illustrate the camera scene. The numerical target summarizes a region of interest, not the colour of one thumbnail. No sponsor endorsement is implied.

The preserved EFI target gzip was retrieved on 26 November 2026. It has SHA-256 `a18320ce2b62976eca922781a9295df056d776534a65ed4501eadf7fc5663601`. The source URL and retrieval time are in [model/source.json](model/source.json). [EFI target definitions](https://projects.ecoforecast.org/neon4cast-docs/Phenology.html) · [NEON phenology camera product DP1.00033.001](https://data.neonscience.org/data-products/DP1.00033.001) · [NEON usage policy](https://www.neonscience.org/usage-policies)

PhenoCam also supplies the camera- and vegetation-specific mask registry, binary TIFF masks and one-day RGB summaries. Frame and metadata URLs, hashes, candidate-mask counts, measured RGB and the matched TIFF are recorded alongside each result. The overlay follows the black (included) TIFF pixels at the original photo dimensions and is resized with nearest-neighbor sampling. The midday frame is a single exposure. The GCC90 and RCC90 targets summarize a daily distribution. A photo’s mean RGB is used solely to identify the sampling area in the retrospective archive viewer. The historical forecast and ranking use their original inputs. [STEI deciduous canopy mask registry](https://phenocam.nau.edu/webcam/roi/NEON.D05.STEI.DP1.00033/DB_1000/) · [Local source record](data/regions.js)
