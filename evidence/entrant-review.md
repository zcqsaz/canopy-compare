# One remote review at STEI

The Codex-built archive workflow paired a spring greening flag at Steigerwaldt-Chequamegon (STEI), Wisconsin, with two dated PhenoCam photographs. The gold outline identifies the deciduous broadleaf canopy region used for measurement. The entrant was asked to compare that area in the two images before comparing the recorded colour values.

**Entrant’s reply:** “看过两张照片，保留提示” (“I looked at both photos. Keep the flag.”) This decision applies to the greening flag for this pair. The accompanying demonstration video uses scripted interface actions.

## Recorded observations checked afterwards

The archived daily green chromatic coordinate (`gcc_90`) rose from 0.35728 on 6 May to 0.44603 on 15 May, a change of +0.08875 between the photo dates.

The 8 May forecast used a four-day baseline of 0.35067, the median for 3–6 May. It predicted 0.39732 on 15 May, a rise of +0.04665 from that baseline. The observed rise from the same baseline was +0.09536. The retained flag therefore matched the observed direction, while the forecast understated the increase.
